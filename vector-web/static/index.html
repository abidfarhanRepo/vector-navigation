      Help the map learn?

      Vector can improve its routing from how you actually drive. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can stop it at any time.

      What is stored

      • Aggregate speeds per stretch of road, per time of week.
      • Nothing until at least 5 separate trips cover the same stretch in the same time band. Below that, the data is deleted unused.

      What is never stored

      • Your identity, account, device or IP — there is no user record at all.
      • The start and end of every trip: the first and last 200 m are discarded on your device, so your home and work are not in the data.
      • Raw positions beyond 72 hours — they are deleted, not archived. Only the aggregates survive, and those describe roads, not people.
      • Anything linking one trip to another: each trip gets a fresh random label that is thrown away when the trip ends.

      Don't take our word for it — see what has been discarded and what the map has learned. Both are live.

      Recording in this tab needs the screen on and the tab in front — a web page cannot record in the background. Other ways to contribute, including uploading a drive your phone already recorded, which needs none of that.

      You can change this later with the same button. Turning it off stops collection immediately; aggregates already contributed cannot be traced back to you, so there is nothing about you left to delete.
